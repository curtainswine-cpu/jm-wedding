Local Guide

Things to Do

Lartington Hall sits on the edge of Teesdale, just outside the market town of Barnard Castle. If you are making a weekend of it, here are some ideas for filling the time, all within easy reach of the Hall.

Close to the Hall

Barnard Castle2 to 3 miles from the Hall

The market town the Hall looks to. Wander the streets, find the octagonal Market Cross, and take your pick of pubs, cafés and restaurants.

 The Bowes MuseumIn Barnard Castle

A magnificent French-style chateau, home to the famous Silver Swan, an eighteenth-century silver automaton.

 Barnard Castle RuinsIn Barnard Castle

The ruins of a twelfth-century castle stand high above the River Tees, looked after by English Heritage.

 Egglestone AbbeyJust outside Barnard Castle

The peaceful ruins of a twelfth-century abbey in a wooded bend of the Tees. Free to visit.

Walks & Fresh Air

Along the River TeesFrom Barnard Castle

A gentle riverside walk links the Bowes Museum with Egglestone Abbey, and the Teesdale Way makes a mostly flat circular route. Sensible shoes recommended.

 The VillagesWithin about 3 miles

Cotherstone, Romaldkirk, Bowes and Startforth are all close by, and make for a lovely countryside amble.

A Little Further Afield

High Force & Low ForceAbout 15 miles from Barnard Castle

Spectacular waterfalls on the River Tees in Upper Teesdale, with a circular walk of around five miles taking in both.

 Raby CastleAbout 8 miles from Barnard Castle

A medieval castle with walled gardens and a deer park, and still a family home. Please check opening arrangements first.

 Hamsterley ForestAbout 10 miles from Barnard Castle

Woodland trails for walking and mountain biking, if you fancy stretching your legs among the trees.

Eating & Drinking

The Old Well InnBarnard Castle

A seventeenth-century pub on The Bank, with cask ales and a beer garden.

 The Cricketers ArmsBarnard Castle

A local on Galgate with a good range of real ale and live sport.
CrobarBarnard Castle

A characterful bar on Horse Market, with craft ales, a strong gin selection and a garden lounge.

Fish & Chips at 149Barnard Castle

A well-established chippy on Galgate and one of the most reviewed places in town.

Café CheesedaleBarnard Castle

The home of Teesdale Cheesemakers, so expect a cheese-led menu.

 The Rose & CrownRomaldkirk

An eighteenth-century coaching inn on the village green, well known for its food.

Tap a card to visit its website.

Opening hours and menus change, so it is worth checking before you set off and booking ahead where you can. More ideas at Visit County Durham.

Getting Around

Taxis are best pre-booked

Barnard Castle is the nearest town, so please book ahead, especially for the evening. Hodgsons Taxis, based in Barnard Castle, take private hire bookings.
