Getting There

Travel & Stay

Lartington Hall sits on the edge of Teesdale, just outside the market town of Barnard Castle in County Durham. Here is everything you need to plan your journey and where to stay.

The Venue

Lartington Hall
Lartington Lane, Barnard Castle
County Durham, DL12 9BW

View on Google Maps →

By Car

Lartington Hall is just outside Barnard Castle. Please use the postcode DL12 9BW in your satnav.

Lartington Hall website →

By Train

Barnard Castle does not have a railway station. The nearest mainline station is Darlington, from where you will need a taxi or a car to reach the Hall.

Taxis

Taxis are best pre-booked, especially for the evening. Hodgsons Taxis, based in Barnard Castle, take bookings on 01833 630730.

hodgsonstaxis.com →

Where to Stay

Barnard Castle and the villages around it have inns and B&Bs close to the Hall. We would recommend booking early.

The Old Well InnBarnard Castle

A seventeenth-century inn on The Bank, with rooms in the heart of the town.

 The Rose & CrownRomaldkirk

An eighteenth-century coaching inn on the village green, with rooms and a well-known restaurant.
