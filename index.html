How it began

OurStory

Our story began in March 2021, although it turns out it could have started much earlier. We were both born in the same hospital just four days apart, but somehow it took 25 years and a swipe on Tinder for our paths to finally cross.

What started as easy conversation and an instant friendship quickly became something much more. We clicked from the very beginning, and before long we realised we simply enjoyed doing life together. In fact, Jessica was so convinced they were “just friends” at first that she even encouraged Matthew to go on another Tinder date. It didn’t take long for her to realise that wasn’t a very good idea after all.

From the beginning, being together felt easy. Matthew’s calm, steady nature brought a sense of peace and comfort, while Jessica’s bubbly, spontaneous personality ensured there was never a dull moment. Together, we somehow balance each other perfectly.

Over the last few years, we’ve filled our lives with adventures, laughter and countless memories. We’ve travelled to Mexico and Florida, cheered on Liverpool, spent far too much time listening to Post Malone, and generally embraced life as two slightly overgrown children who refuse to take themselves too seriously.

Along the way, our family grew too. Margot joined Jessica’s life just before we met, and a year later we welcomed Mabel. Our two Shar Peis spend most of their time play-fighting, stealing space in the bed and causing chaos, but they are our first babies and we couldn’t love them more.

In true fairy-tale fashion, Matthew proposed during a trip to Disney World at the Grand Floridian, a place Jessica had dreamed about since she was a little girl. Completely unaware of what was about to happen, she happily posed for a photo with a Cinderella glass slipper after a cast member suggested Matthew get down on one knee for the picture. Taking advantage of the perfect opportunity, he asked for “just one more photo” before producing a ring instead.

The rest of the evening was spent calling our favourite people, celebrating our engagement, and watching the Disney fireworks with our Mickey and Minnie ears firmly in place.

Now, we can’t wait for our next chapter. We are excited to build a family, grow old together, continue our adventures, and create a lifetime of memories. Most of all, we can’t wait to celebrate with all the people we love as we finally say “I do”.

For us, marriage means choosing your best friend every single day, and we feel incredibly lucky that we found each other.
