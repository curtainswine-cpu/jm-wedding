The Wedding Party

The Maid of Honour

Kirsten
Maid of Honour
We started out serving McDonald’s and somehow ended up here! My nurse, my dog-sitter and now my maid of honour – Kirsten has certainly worked her way up the ranks! From serving nuggets to helping me get down the aisle, she’s been promoted quite a few times over the years. She’s my soul mate, my partner in crime and one of the very few people who knows absolutely everything about me and still chooses to stick around… questionable judgement, really. She’s seen me at my best, my worst and probably a few states in between that should never be spoken about again. There’s no one else I’d want by my side for the biggest day of my life – although after all these years, she’s probably legally entitled to a share of the wedding budget! Love you forever, you absolute liability.

The Bridesmaids

Georgina
Bridesmaid
My little sister, professional eye-roller and queen of the sass. Never short of an opinion, rarely impressed by anything I do, and always ready with a look that says more than a thousand words ever could. She’s got a law degree, a sharp tongue and absolutely no intention of letting me get away with anything. Wouldn’t have her any other way, love her always.
Jessica
Bridesmaid
Single and ready to mingle, Jess has a very specific type… grey joggers, a snapback and a bit of N-Dubz. If he looks like he’s just stepped out of a Dappy music video, she’s already interested. So gents, consider this your warning – Jess is on the bridesmaid lineup and officially on the market.
Sarah
Bridesmaid
A walking contradiction: equal parts sensible adult and absolute liability. One minute she’s the voice of reason, the next she’s reliving her teenage years to McFly, Busted, S Club and Steps. You never quite know which Sarah you’re going to get, but one thing’s for sure – she’s a fantastic mum and has successfully kept a tiny human alive, so she can’t be doing too badly!
Hannah
Bridesmaid
Don’t let the quiet, reserved exterior fool you. Give this girl a few drinks and a microphone and suddenly she’s the main event. First on the karaoke, last off the dance floor, and absolutely convinced she’s the next superstar. The crown is permanent, but the volume depends on the alcohol. Long live the queen.
Melody
Bridesmaid
Pretty face, dangerous after a few drinks. Full-time teacher, full-time mum and usually the sensible one… until the alcohol kicks in. Then all bets are off and the night has officially taken a turn. There’s only one way to know Mel’s had a really good night – if she ends up being sick in a bath! She might be responsible for a living, but get enough drinks in her and all that goes straight out the window!
Molly
Bridesmaid
Irish, loud as hell and about as chaotic as they come – but with a heart so big it could take over the whole room. Molly loves hard, drinks hard, and sings and dances like nobody’s watching (even when absolutely everyone is). A gorgeous ray of sunshine who leaves a little bit of magic everywhere she goes. She might be a handful, but she’s our handful, and the world is a better place with her in it. Just don’t expect a quiet night when Molly’s around!

Parents of the Couple

Rebecca
Mother of the Bride
My Mumma Bear, my biggest cheerleader and forever my home, no matter how many houses we live in. Always on hand whenever she’s needed and loving her family harder than anyone I know. She’s been there for every chapter of my life, and I wouldn’t be who I am without her. Now she gets the very special job of helping me through the biggest one yet – and probably making sure I’ve eaten, got everything I need and haven’t forgotten anything along the way. I’m forever lucky that I get to call her my mum.
Anthony
Father of the Bride
My dad, my protector, my biggest supporter and the man who gets the honour of walking me down the aisle. From childhood memories and family adventures to all the moments that have made me who I am, he’s always been there. Whether it’s a bit of sport, a good stout or chasing the next thrill, he’s never one to sit still for too long! I’ll always be his little girl, and no matter how grown up I get or whose surname I take, I’ll always be his daughter. There’s no one else I’d rather have by my side for that walk.
