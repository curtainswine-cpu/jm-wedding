Good to Know

Questions

Where is Lartington Hall?

Lartington Hall, Lartington Lane, Barnard Castle, County Durham, DL12 9BW. It sits just outside the market town of Barnard Castle. You will find a map on the Travel page.

What is the dress code?

Black tie. We’d love to see you dressed to the nines.

Are children invited?

Your invitation will show exactly who is included.

Can I bring a plus one?

Your invitation will show the number of seats reserved in your name.

What time should I arrive?

Guests are welcome from 11:45 am for a ‘getting comfy’ half hour. Please be seated by 12:15 pm, as the ceremony begins at 12:30 pm. Evening guests are welcome from 6:30 pm.

What is the nearest train station?

Barnard Castle does not have a railway station. The nearest mainline station is Darlington, so you will need a taxi or a car from there to reach the Hall. Taxis are best pre-booked: you will find a local firm, Hodgsons Taxis, at the bottom of the Things to Do page.

Where should I stay?

Barnard Castle is the nearest town, and has inns and B&Bs close by. The Old Well Inn in town and the Rose & Crown in Romaldkirk both offer rooms.

How do I get home after the evening?

The bar closes and the music finishes at midnight. Taxis are best pre-booked, so please arrange your return journey in advance. Hodgsons Taxis in Barnard Castle take bookings on 01833 630730, and you will find their details at the bottom of the Things to Do page.
